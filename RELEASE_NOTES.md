# Tarn 0.1.0

A tarn is a small, still mountain lake. Tarn is a calm place to focus, and to breathe.

First release. A free, open source Mac app made by Antelis Labs, no account and no tracking.

- **Focus:** Pomodoro rounds with a glowing circle, round dots, calm breaks, daily stats, an optional schedule and ten color themes
- **Breathe:** a flower or water that opens and closes with your breath, four patterns, gentle prompts and 1 to 20 minute sessions
- **Calm mode:** full screen, sound and Hide apps in one button, with one switch to turn all of that off
- **Sounds:** four recorded ambient loops, off until you choose them
- `tarnctl` command line tool and `tarn://` links for Shortcuts and Raycast
- macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon and Intel
