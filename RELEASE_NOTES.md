# Tarn 0.2.0

A small floating circle, and a clearer installer.

- New, optional floating circle: a small frosted circle that stays above your other windows. It shows the Focus timer, or the breathing picture in Breathe. Turn it on in Settings, Focus. Four sizes, adjustable opacity, drag it anywhere, and a speaker button on hover to switch sound on or off. It is off by default and steps aside in full screen
- The installer window now says exactly what to do: double click Tarn and choose Move to Applications, or drag Tarn onto Applications

## Tarn 0.1.0

A tarn is a small, still mountain lake. Tarn is a calm place to focus, and to breathe.

First release. A free, open source Mac app made by Antelis Labs, no account and no tracking.

- **Focus:** Pomodoro rounds with a glowing circle, round dots, calm breaks, daily stats, an optional schedule and ten color themes
- **Breathe:** a flower or water that opens and closes with your breath, four rhythms, optional written prompts and 1 to 20 minute sessions. A soft sound plays by default and is easy to turn off; pausing pauses the sound too
- **Calm mode:** full screen, sound and Hide apps in one button, with one switch to turn all of that off
- **Sounds:** four recorded ambient loops. Silent in Focus until you turn them on, soft by default in Breathe
- `tarnctl` command line tool and `tarn://` links for Shortcuts and Raycast
- macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon and Intel
- **Help:** a searchable FAQ in the app, plus optional tips (off by default)
- **Install:** drag to Applications, or open Tarn from the disk image and let it move itself. One click installs `tarnctl` from Settings
- A quick actions menu in the window, and a daily schedule with a one tap Workday preset
- **Check for updates:** a button in Settings. Tarn only goes online when you press it, and it sends nothing about you
- Never speaks and never collects anything
